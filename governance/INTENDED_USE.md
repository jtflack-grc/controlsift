# Intended Use

ControlSift is intended for:

1. **Research** on whether QLoRA domain adaptation improves small-LM evidence-quality classification.
2. **Education / portfolio demonstration** of experimental design, PEFT, evaluation, and AI assurance artifacts.
3. **Method comparison** among majority, TF-IDF, zero-shot, few-shot, and QLoRA on a controlled synthetic benchmark.

Human judgment remains authoritative for any real control evidence.

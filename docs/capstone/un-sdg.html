ControlSift

    UN Sustainable Development Goals

    ControlSift is scoped as a UN SDG project build under Mentor Me Collective’s global-futures residency model. The work strengthens institutional accountability around cybersecurity evidence — and keeps responsible AI practice inspectable.

    Primary goal

    SDG 16 · Peace, justice & strong institutions

    Accountable institutions need honest evidence

    Organizations certify controls with artifacts that often fail to prove those controls operate. Confusing policy language with operational proof weakens audits, certifications, and public trust. ControlSift measures whether a small language model can support human reviewers in distinguishing sufficient, partial, insufficient, irrelevant, and contradictory evidence — under a sealed protocol that treats false assurance as a first-class risk.

    Focus areas: accountable institutions (16.6) and informed oversight culture (16.10) through better evidence hygiene.

    Contribution

    Reduce automation bias in assurance workflows

    The project pairs model development with a Failure Lab, prohibited-use boundaries, and public metrics that stay empty until experiments actually run. The goal is triage support that makes institutional claims more honest — not an automated auditor that rubber-stamps paperwork.

    Supporting goals

    SDG 9 · Industry, innovation & infrastructure

    Research methods that run on free GPU tiers

    Training and evaluation are designed for accessible compute (Kaggle / Colab) using QLoRA and 4-bit quantization, so adaptation research remains reproducible without enterprise infrastructure.

    SDG 4 · Quality education

    Open, curriculum-aligned research craft

    The DeepMind AI Research Foundations pathway is demonstrated through a public research site: data and model cards, sealed evaluation, classical baselines, and structured reflection.

    Impact statement

    If small models can be adapted to flag weak evidence without claiming certainty, assurance teams gain a triage aid that strengthens institutional honesty. ControlSift measures that possibility under a locked protocol and publishes where the approach still fails.

    UN SDG / Global Futures scope

    Mentor Me Collective frames residencies around Google-grade rigor and UN SDG project builds (global good-works / Global Futures Education scope). This page maps ControlSift honestly — no decorative goal-stapling.

    Rule ControlSift’s public identity is research integrity for evidence quality — not a UN campaign site. SDG mapping lives here so MMC evaluators can see the fit without rewriting the product.

    Primary alignment

    SDG 16 · Peace, justice & strong institutions

    Institutional accountability

    Security and GRC teams routinely confuse paperwork with proof. Weak evidence triage undermines audits, certifications, and public trust in institutions that claim control effectiveness. ControlSift studies whether small models can assist honest triage — and documents where they must not replace humans.

    Targets: 16.6 (accountable institutions), 16.10 (access to information / public oversight culture) via better evidence hygiene — not “AI law enforcement.”

    Why this is not a stretch

    False assurance is a governance failure mode

    When models rubber-stamp policy PDFs as “sufficient,” they accelerate institutional theater. The project’s Failure Lab, prohibited-use list, and null-until-run metrics are the SDG-relevant controls: reduce automation bias in assurance workflows.

    Secondary alignment

    SDG 9 · Industry, innovation & infrastructure

    Accessible applied AI capability

    Methods are designed to run on free GPU tiers (Kaggle/Colab) with QLoRA / 4-bit acceleration — so resource-constrained teams can reproduce research-grade adaptation work without enterprise clusters.

    SDG 4 · Quality education

    Curriculum-visible craft

    The DeepMind AI Research Foundations pathway is implemented as a public, inspectable research artifact (not a private homework dump): data cards, sealed evaluation, classical baselines, and explicit reflection.

    What we refuse to claim

    • Not SDG 13 climate theater — cybersecurity evidence quality is not a climate project; we will not force that mapping.
    • Not a UN endorsement — alignment is project-scope fit for MMC, not affiliation.
    • Not production GRC automation — deploying this as an auditor would work against SDG 16 by manufacturing false assurance.
    • Not poverty / health / hunger decoration — if a rubric demands those, we decline the stretch and keep 16/9/4.

    Impact thesis (one slide)

    If small models can be adapted to flag weak evidence without claiming certainty, then under-resourced assurance teams gain a triage aid that strengthens institutional honesty. ControlSift’s job is to measure that “if” under a sealed protocol — and publish failures.

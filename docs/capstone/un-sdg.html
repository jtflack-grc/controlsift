ControlSift

    UN Sustainable Development Goals

    GCLP Capstone Project Outline requires selecting one official goal from Goals 1, 2, 3, 10, or 13. ControlSift’s official submission goal is SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities). Supporting context below does not replace that selection.

    Official GCLP goal

    SDG 10 · Reduced inequalities

    Unequal access to trustworthy assurance expertise

    High-quality governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) review is expensive. Well-resourced organizations buy deep human assurance; smaller teams, nonprofits, clinics, schools, and community providers often inherit template binders and rubber-stamp evidence. That inequality shows up as false assurance: paperwork that looks compliant while controls do not operate.

    ControlSift studies whether a small, free-tier-trainable language model can help human reviewers triage evidence quality—sufficient, partial, insufficient, irrelevant, contradictory—so under-resourced teams gain access to a research-grade triage aid they could not otherwise afford. The model never replaces the human reviewer; it aims to reduce the expertise gap that currently tracks wealth and consulting access.

    GCLP cybersecurity example for Goal 10 emphasizes empowering communities with unequal digital protection. ControlSift applies that lens to unequal access to honest evidence review.

    Contribution

    Lower the barrier without automating away judgment

    The deliverable is an open research artifact: sealed synthetic benchmark, classical baselines, planned Gemma 3 + QLoRA adaptation on free GPU tiers, Failure Lab, and Assurance docs. Success means a reproducible method that smaller organizations and learners can inspect—not a paid black-box auditor.

    Problem identification sources (public evidence)

    Per GCLP guidance to ground the problem in real-world evidence (while the modeling corpus itself remains synthetic for privacy and leakage control), problem framing draws on public sources such as:

    • Verizon DBIR / industry breach reports — recurring control failures where documentation did not equal operation.
    • CISA / NIST cybersecurity guidance — evidence and control assessment expectations for organizations of uneven maturity.
    • Public audit and certification discourse — known tension between “paper compliance” and operational proof.
    • Kaggle / open security datasets — used as landscape context for the domain; not as private customer evidence.

    Modeling uses a sealed synthetic packet benchmark so no employer, patient, or customer evidence enters the repo. That is a deliberate privacy and integrity choice, disclosed in the Data Card.

    Supporting context (not the official selection)

    SDG 16 · Strong institutions

    Mechanism of impact

    Honest evidence triage strengthens institutional accountability. This explains how reducing inequality in assurance access works; it is not the GCLP official goal selection.

    SDG 9 & 4

    Access & learning

    Free-tier PEFT methods (SDG 9 adjacent) and a curriculum-visible public site (SDG 4 adjacent) support accessibility of the research craft.

    Impact statement

    If small models can help flag weak evidence under a locked protocol, organizations with less GRC budget gain a triage aid that makes institutional claims more honest. ControlSift measures that possibility and publishes where the approach still fails—so the benefit is shared as knowledge, not sold as certainty.

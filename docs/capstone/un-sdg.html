ControlSift

    Reduced Inequalities: rubric label vs. UN designation

    The MMC capstone rubric labels Reduced Inequalities as its program Goal 4 option. In the official United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, Reduced Inequalities is SDG 10. To avoid ambiguity, ControlSift uses both labels together: MMC rubric option 4 — Reduced Inequalities / official UN SDG 10.

    Capstone alignment

    MMC option 4 · UN SDG 10

    Unequal access to cybersecurity capacity

    NIST explicitly notes that smaller businesses often operate with limited cybersecurity resources and budgets and may be unable to afford dedicated cybersecurity staff. ControlSift does not claim that this proves a specific shortage of control-evidence reviewers; it uses the broader resource constraint as the motivation to study an open, low-cost assurance-support method.

    The project asks whether a lightweight model can contribute to evidence-quality triage while keeping a human reviewer authoritative. The artifact, benchmark, methods, failure analysis, and results are public so the research can be inspected without buying a proprietary platform.

    Contribution

    Lower the research barrier, not the assurance standard

    ControlSift runs on free-tier or commodity research infrastructure, uses synthetic data rather than customer evidence, and publishes a classical baseline alongside the language-model experiments. Its strongest result is not an AI victory: TF-IDF leads the full model ladder.

    The long-term idea is scalable knowledge access: publish methods, limitations, and failure cases that smaller teams and learners can inspect. Production deployment remains out of scope without real-world validation and accountable human review.

    Public evidence behind the framing

    • NIST Small Business Cybersecurity Corner NIST states that smaller businesses have limited resources and budgets and need practical, actionable, cost-effective cybersecurity guidance and solutions. Source
    • NIST: Building Your Small Business' Cybersecurity Team NIST discusses limited resources, inability to afford dedicated staff, and the use of outsourcing, upskilling, and community support. Source
    • NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5 NIST's control-assessment methodology centers on determining whether controls are implemented and achieve intended outcomes, grounding ControlSift's distinction between paperwork and operational proof. Source
    • United Nations Goal 10 The UN's official Goal 10 is Reduced Inequalities. The connection between that broad goal and lower-cost cybersecurity assurance research is ControlSift's capstone interpretation, not a claim that the UN specifically addresses evidence review. Source

    What the experiment contributes

    Open method

    Reproducible on modest infrastructure

    The benchmark generator, model ladder, validation scripts, results, Failure Lab, Data Card, Model Card, and risk register are public. The QLoRA leg used a lightweight Gemma 3 1B model and free GPU infrastructure.

    Honest result

    Do not make access cheaper by making assurance weaker

    The sealed result does not justify automated evidence approval. TF-IDF leads at about 0.53 test macro F1; the best Gemma rung is few-shot at about 0.14. ControlSift therefore publishes the failure rather than presenting a weak model as a low-cost substitute for professional judgment.

    Supporting context, not the official selection

    SDG 16 · Strong institutions

    Accountability as a secondary connection

    Better evidence discipline can support institutional accountability. This is useful context for the project, but it is not the capstone's selected rubric option.

    SDG 9 & 4

    Accessible methods and learning

    Efficient fine-tuning and a public, curriculum-visible research artifact are adjacent to innovation and education. Again, these are supporting connections rather than the formal capstone selection.

    Impact statement

    ControlSift's contribution is deliberately modest: test whether a low-cost small-model approach adds value to cybersecurity evidence triage, publish the full result even when the model underperforms, and make the method inspectable. The project aligns that open-access research goal with MMC's Reduced Inequalities option while retaining the official UN designation, SDG 10.

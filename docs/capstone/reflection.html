    Reflection

    Capstone reflection is incomplete until Gemma runs land — and that incompleteness is stated plainly. Classical-stage lessons already changed the science.

    What already changed the work

    • TF-IDF saturation at 1.0 forced a generator redesign. Lexical shortcuts were not a footnote — they were the first result.
    • Compositional v1.1 surface made the benchmark harder for bag-of-words (~0.53 macro F1) and introduced a disclosed schema-reading risk.
    • Protocol lock before LLM claims — prompt and test hashes frozen; HEAD may still advance docs without moving the seal.
    • Label-audit honesty — we stopped implying “human review done” when the pass was structural/scripted.

    What we still do not know (pending GPU)

    • Whether zero-shot Gemma discovers packet grammar without tutoring
    • Whether few-shot beats zero-shot on sealed test / challenge
    • Whether QLoRA improves macro F1 enough to matter after uncertainty
    • Which failure modes are new versus classical residual errors

    If adaptation underperforms, that remains a valid MMC / research outcome.

    What the benchmark still fails to represent

    • Multi-document evidence binders and real OCR noise
    • Organization-specific control language and politics of audit negotiation
    • Fully human gold labels at scale
    • Live deployment risk under time pressure (by scope exclusion)

    What should be studied next

    • Complete Gemma ladder on free GPU; refresh Failure Lab with LLM misses
    • If schema gaming dominates, schedule prose-hardened dataset v1.2 with a new protocol tag
    • Optional human GRC spot-pass on a small challenge sample — labeled as such

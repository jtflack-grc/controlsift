    Tight script (~4:30)

    Read aloud as written. Pause only at beat breaks. ~620 words ≈ 4:20 at a calm pace.

    0:00–0:35 · Hook
    “I’m John Flack presenting ControlSift, my Mentor Me Collective DeepMind AI Research Foundations capstone. Official SDG: Goal 10, Reduced Inequalities. The question: can a small language model help teams with less GRC budget tell proof from paperwork?”
    0:35–1:15 · Problem
    “High-quality assurance review is expensive. Smaller organizations often inherit binders that look compliant when controls don’t operate. That inequality is what I’m targeting—not by replacing auditors, but by researching an open triage aid.”
    1:15–2:10 · Methods
    “Methods: a sealed synthetic benchmark of 1,500 five-class evidence packets, family-isolated splits, and a frozen evaluation protocol. The ladder is majority class, TF-IDF logistic regression, then Gemma 3 1B zero-shot, few-shot, and QLoRA on a free-tier GPU path. Primary metric is macro F1 on the sealed TEST set.”
    2:10–3:25 · Results (real TEST numbers)
    “On sealed TEST, majority macro F1 is about 0.067. TF-IDF still leads at about 0.533 after we hardened the dataset so bag-of-words could not cheat. The Gemma 3 1B ladder is weaker in absolute terms: zero-shot about 0.080; few-shot best of the LLM runs at about 0.137; QLoRA about 0.083—but QLoRA label-parse success is only about 44%, so that score is fragile. Honest takeaway: lexical TF-IDF wins this benchmark; the small LLM ladder is a research baseline, not a replacement. Every number is from the published result files—no invented scores.”
    3:25–4:05 · Responsible AI
    “Responsible innovation: synthetic data only, Data Card, Risk Register, Failure Lab, and a clear Intended Use—research and education, not a production auditor. The curriculum map shows the DeepMind path end to end.”
    4:05–4:30 · Close
    “Results and figures are live on the public GitHub Pages site. Capstone report, slides, and this walkthrough close the MMC submission pack. Thanks for reviewing.”

    If over time: cut the Problem beat to two sentences and skip “curriculum map” in Responsible AI. If under 4:00: add one sentence on family-isolated splits under Methods.

    “High-quality assurance review is expensive. Smaller organizations often inherit binders that look compliant when controls don’t operate. That inequality is the problem I’m targeting—not by replacing auditors, but by researching an open triage aid.”

    “Methods: a sealed synthetic benchmark of 1,500 five-class evidence packets, family-isolated splits, and a frozen evaluation protocol. Ladder: majority, TF-IDF, then Gemma 3 1B zero-shot, few-shot, and QLoRA on a free-tier GPU path. Primary metric is macro F1.”

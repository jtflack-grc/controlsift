    Evaluation controls

    • Family isolation: scenario families stay within one split to reduce leakage.
    • Machine-readable metrics: public scores come from committed result JSON.
    • Malformed outputs: invalid Gemma labels count as errors rather than being silently repaired.
    • Failure analysis: aggregate scores are paired with concrete hard cases in the Failure Lab.
    • Version disclosure: the public site never presents v1.1 classical and v1.0 Gemma as a controlled same-benchmark ranking.

    One-paragraph pitch (for MMC)

    ControlSift studies whether QLoRA adaptation of Gemma 3 1B improves five-class judgment of cybersecurity evidence quality on a sealed synthetic benchmark. The work demonstrates DeepMind foundations (data representation, transformers, fine-tuning, acceleration, alignment/responsible innovation) while targeting UN SDG 16 — stronger institutional accountability through honest evidence triage — without claiming a production auditor or inventing LLM scores before GPU runs.

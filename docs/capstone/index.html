    Summary

    ControlSift asks whether QLoRA adaptation of Gemma 3 1B improves five-class judgment of cybersecurity evidence quality on a sealed synthetic benchmark. The project applies DeepMind foundations — data representation, transformers, fine-tuning, acceleration, and responsible innovation — to a UN SDG 16 problem: helping institutions distinguish proof from paperwork without replacing human judgment.

    Current results

    • Complete — Benchmark v1.1, protocol lock, majority baseline (macro F1 0.067), TF-IDF (~0.53 test), Assurance hub, Failure Lab, label audit.
    • In progress — Gemma zero-shot, few-shot, and QLoRA metrics (public values remain unavailable until GPU runs finish).

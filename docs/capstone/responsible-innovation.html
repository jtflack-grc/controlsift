    Responsible innovation

    DeepMind foundations treat responsible innovation as central — not a final slide. ControlSift’s Assurance hub is the living artifact.

    Controls against false assurance

    • Null-until-run metrics — public site refuses invented Gemma scores.
    • Protocol lock — no test-set prompt shopping after seal.
    • Label honesty — rule-derived labels; structural audit + scripted spot-check; never “fully human-labeled.”
    • Research surface disclosure — compositional packets may enable schema reading; we say so.
    • Prohibited uses — not a production auditor; human oversight required for any real evidence.
    • Negative findings preserved — if QLoRA fails, that remains the result.

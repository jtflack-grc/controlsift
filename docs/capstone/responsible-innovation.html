ControlSift

    Responsible innovation

    Responsible-AI practice is built into the project through scope, evidence, failure visibility, and explicit limits on automation and claims.

    Design choices

    • Metrics from files only: scores are surfaced from committed experiment artifacts rather than invented presentation values.
    • Label provenance: labels are rule-derived with documented audit coverage and aren't presented as a large human gold standard.
    • Privacy boundary: synthetic organizations and evidence only; no employer, patient, or customer evidence.
    • Human oversight: real-world use would require accountable human review; ControlSift isn't an automated auditor.
    • Negative findings: within Gemma v1.0, QLoRA didn't beat few-shot and its low parse-success rate remains visible.
    • Version honesty: classical v1.1 and Gemma v1.0 scores aren't presented as a controlled same-benchmark ranking.

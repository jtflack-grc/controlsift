ControlSift

    Intended use

    What ControlSift is for, and what it is not for.

    Intended

    • Research Whether QLoRA improves small-LM evidence-quality classification on a controlled benchmark.
    • Education / portfolio Experimental design, PEFT, evaluation, and AI assurance artifacts.
    • Method comparison Majority, TF-IDF, zero-shot, few-shot, and QLoRA under one protocol.

    Not intended

    • Production audit decisions Human judgment remains authoritative for real control evidence.
    • Compliance certification No regulatory or attestation use.
    • Live evidence ingestion No document upload, OCR, or RAG product in v1.

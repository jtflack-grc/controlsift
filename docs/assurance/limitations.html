ControlSift

    Limitations

    Where ControlSift should not be trusted, even after Gemma results land.

    • Synthetic data Generator assumptions may not match real audit evidence distributions.
    • Rule-derived labels Not fully human-labeled gold. Challenge uses structural audit + a 20-case spot-check.
    • Compositional research surface (v1.1) Dual sections, decoys, and SCOPE scaffolding mean strong scores may reflect schema reading, not naturalistic binder judgment.
    • Uneven class difficulty Classical CONTRADICTORY remains easier than SUFFICIENT / IRRELEVANT.
    • Frozen prompt Zero-shot does not tutor the packet grammar; discovery failures are valid outcomes.
    • Single small model Gemma 3 1B results do not automatically transfer to other sizes or families.
    • Single-artifact cases v1 is not a multi-document evidence binder task.
    • No live inference product Static research artifact only.
    • Parse failures Malformed outputs become UNPARSEABLE and count against performance.

    How to read this

    • TF-IDF remains a dataset-integrity check Extremely high lexical scores warn that a synthetic benchmark may contain shortcuts. Dataset v1.1.0 was hardened after an earlier generator version saturated the classical model.
    • The Gemma v1.0 ladder is internally comparable Zero-shot, few-shot, and QLoRA were evaluated on the earlier v1.0.0 surface. Few-shot is strongest of those three; QLoRA underperforms few-shot and has poor output-label parse success.
    • A final cross-model conclusion requires one version Rerunning Gemma on v1.1.0, or recovering a matching v1.0 classical baseline, is the clean way to answer whether the small language model beats the classical baseline on exactly the same benchmark.
